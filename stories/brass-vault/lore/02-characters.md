**Mags Holloway**
Mags Holloway is the leader of the crew, a broad-shouldered woman in her fifties with a captain's coat gone grey at the seams and a brass hook where her left hand used to be. She waits in the back room of The Rusty Gear Tavern with the plan of the bank pinned to the wall.
- **Personality:** dry, patient, never raises her voice; treats the heist like sailing through a storm.
- **Wants:** the Clockheart kept out of the Admiralty's hands, and revenge on Silas Ashcombe, who seized her airship over a single missed payment.
- **Knows:** the layout of the bank; that the Orrery Lock is set to "the founding night" but not the exact positions; that Captain Juno Vale will wait at Mooring Mast Nine only until dawn; that Pip Quillon knows the pneumatic tubes and that Tobias Fenn sells the right tools.
- **Secret:** she plans to return the Clockheart to Professor Amara Quell's daughter in the colonies, not to sell it. She has told no one.
- **Speech:** short sentences, sailing metaphors ("trim your sails", "dead calm before the squall").

**Pip Quillon**
Pip Quillon is a fourteen-year-old runner and pickpocket in a cap two sizes too big, usually found on Lamplight Row leaning on the pneumatic post box.
- **Personality:** cheeky, fast-talking, brave until things get loud, then gone.
- **Wants:** enough money to rent a room for herself and her little brother; respect from the grown-ups.
- **Knows:** every pneumatic tube in Vellmoor; a capsule posted on Lamplight Row reaches the terminal behind the head teller's desk in the Grand Hall in under a minute; the iron hatch on Lamplight Row leads down to the Old Sewer Culvert and the culvert reaches the bank's boiler room; Constable Edda Rourke takes a tea break at half past eleven.
- **Secret:** she once lifted a wallet from Silas Ashcombe himself and still has his calling card.
- **Speech:** street slang, calls everyone "guv" or "missus", talks with her hands.

**Tobias Fenn**
Tobias Fenn is a thin, elderly clockmaker with magnifying lenses clipped to his spectacles, the owner of Fenn's Clockworks in Brassworks Alley. He is also the quietest fence in Vellmoor.
- **Personality:** polite, precise, pedantic about craftsmanship; despises cheap work and loud people.
- **Wants:** payment up front (the tuning fork costs five crowns, the lockpick set three, the grappling hook two), or a favour he can call in later.
- **Knows:** he helped build Sentinel Unit Seven twenty years ago; a struck tuning fork of blued steel makes it pause for a few seconds; a surge of steam pressure confuses it for a full minute; the director's brass punch card makes it obey completely.
- **Secret:** he was Professor Amara Quell's apprentice and secretly helped her finish the Clockheart.
- **Speech:** formal and slow, corrects grammar, compares people to badly made clocks.

**Constable Edda Rourke**
Constable Edda Rourke is a tall, weathered officer of the city watch who patrols Ashcombe Square with a lantern, a truncheon and a whistle.
- **Personality:** honest, stubborn, suspicious of anyone who smiles too much; cannot be bribed with money.
- **Wants:** a quiet night; she would also love to see Silas Ashcombe ruined, because the bank evicted her mother's bakery.
- **Knows:** the gala guest list is checked at the doors; carriages leave at midnight; something is wrong with the bank's books.
- **Secret:** she has been collecting evidence of the bank's fraud and lacks only the Founders' Ledger to prove it; she might look the other way for someone who brings it to her.
- **Speech:** blunt, official ("Move along", "State your business"), softens only when talking about her mother.

**Silas Ashcombe**
Silas Ashcombe is the director of the Ashcombe Mercantile Bank, a silver-haired man in a velvet evening coat with a gold pocket watch, greeting guests in the Grand Hall of the bank until midnight.
- **Personality:** charming in public, cold in private; collects people's debts the way others collect stamps.
- **Wants:** a flawless gala and a record price for the Clockheart at noon.
- **Knows:** the safe combination in his office is the founding year, 1823; he carries no card on him tonight, the brass punch card is locked in the wall safe; the vault alarm rings in the ballroom.
- **Secret:** the bank is nearly bankrupt; without the Admiralty sale it collapses within a month.
- **Speech:** smooth and flattering, uses the listener's name too often, never says "no" directly.

**Horace Pell**
Horace Pell is a young, nervous junior clerk with ink-stained cuffs, working late at his desk in the Clerks' Gallery because he was not invited to the gala.
- **Personality:** anxious, bookish, desperate to be noticed; loves astronomy.
- **Wants:** a way out of the bank; he dreams of joining the Royal Observatory.
- **Knows:** the Orrery Lock positions by heart from the Founders' Ledger: the Comet beside the Third Moon, the Iron Planet at its highest point, the Pale Planet hidden behind the Sun; where the Founders' Ledger sits on the shelf.
- **Secret:** he has been forging small entries in the ledgers on Silas Ashcombe's orders and is terrified of being blamed.
- **Speech:** stammers, over-explains, gets excited and fluent when talking about the stars.

**Bertha Koss**
Bertha Koss is the chief engineer of the Boiler Undercroft, a stout woman with forearms like hams, soot on her face and a wrench in her belt.
- **Personality:** loud, funny, proud of her boilers; fiercely loyal to the stokers who work under her.
- **Wants:** the overdue wages the bank owes her stokers; she would happily "test the main valve" tonight for anyone who promises to get them paid.
- **Knows:** the steam valve key turns the main pressure valve; a sharp surge confuses every automaton in the building for a minute and rattles the radiators in the ballroom; the grated drain in the floor leads to the Old Sewer Culvert.
- **Secret:** she keeps a second steam valve key on a nail behind boiler number three.
- **Speech:** booming, swears creatively by "boilers and brimstone", laughs at danger.

**Sentinel Unit Seven**
Sentinel Unit Seven is a seven-foot clockwork guard of brass and iron that stands beside the great round door in the Orrery Vault Antechamber. Its face is a smooth brass mask with two glowing green lenses; a slot in its chest accepts punched brass cards.
- **Behaviour:** it challenges anyone who enters with a single phrase: "PRESENT CARD." Without the director's brass punch card it raises an alarm and blocks the vault door with its body. It does not fight unless attacked.
- **Weaknesses:** the director's brass punch card makes it obey completely; a struck tuning fork of blued steel makes it pause for a few seconds; a steam surge from the Boiler Undercroft confuses it for a minute.
- **Speech:** speaks only in clipped capital phrases: "PRESENT CARD", "UNAUTHORISED", "ALARM PROTOCOL ENGAGED", "CARD ACCEPTED. AWAITING ORDERS."

**Captain Juno Vale**
Captain Juno Vale is the young captain of the airship Wren's Promise, waiting at the top of Mooring Mast Nine with goggles pushed up on her forehead and a pistol she has never fired.
- **Personality:** reckless, cheerful, mercenary, secretly romantic about the sky.
- **Wants:** fifty crowns for passage, paid on boarding; she leaves at dawn whether the crew is aboard or not.
- **Knows:** the Admiralty patrol ship passes the masts every two hours; the Wren's Promise can outrun it only in fog.
- **Secret:** she owes the Ashcombe bank money too, and the bank has offered to cancel her debt if she reports any suspicious passengers tonight. She has not decided yet.
- **Speech:** airship slang ("cast off", "full burn"), teases everyone, turns serious about money.
