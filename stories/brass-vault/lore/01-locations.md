**The Rusty Gear Tavern**
A low, smoke-stained tavern at the bottom of Lamplight Row, where dockworkers drink bitter ale under a ceiling of rusted gears salvaged from a wrecked airship. Behind the bar a curtain hides the back room, where Mags Holloway has pinned a hand-drawn plan of the Ashcombe Mercantile Bank to the wall. A brass lantern hangs over the table, and a crate in the corner holds the crew's supplies.
- **What to take:** brass lantern (shines a steady light for six hours); smoke canister (fills a room with thick grey smoke for a minute); forged gala invitation (lets one guest through the bank's front doors tonight)
- **Who to meet:** Mags Holloway
- **Where to go next:** Up the stairs to the street → Lamplight Row

**Lamplight Row**
A steep cobbled street lined with gas lamps that never go out. Steam hisses from grates in the pavement, newspaper boys shout the evening edition, and a rusted iron hatch near the gutter leads down into the old sewers. Halfway up the street, a pneumatic post box with a dented brass mouth swallows letters with a thump.
- **What to take:** discarded evening newspaper (tonight's headline: "Clockheart to be Sold to the Admiralty at Noon")
- **Who to meet:** Pip Quillon
- **Where to go next:** Down the stairs → The Rusty Gear Tavern; Uphill → Ashcombe Square; Through a narrow archway → Brassworks Alley; Down the iron hatch → Old Sewer Culvert

**Brassworks Alley**
A crooked alley of workshops where the air tastes of hot metal. Sparks fly from a grinding wheel, and every window is crowded with clocks, springs and half-built mechanical birds. At the end of the alley stands Fenn's Clockworks, a narrow shop with a bell that plays three notes when the door opens.
- **What to take:** lockpick set (opens simple locks and desk drawers); tuning fork of blued steel (when struck near a clockwork automaton, its humming makes the machine pause for a few seconds); grappling hook with a coil of silk rope
- **Who to meet:** Tobias Fenn
- **Where to go next:** Back through the archway → Lamplight Row

**Ashcombe Square**
A wide square paved with white stone, the cleanest place in Vellmoor. Carriages unload guests in silk and furs in front of the bank. In the middle of the square stands the Founder's Clocktower, whose face shows not the time but the positions of the planets. A bronze plaque at its base reads: "Founded on the night the Comet stood beside the Third Moon." Constables patrol the edge of the square with lanterns and whistles.
- **What to take:** none
- **Who to meet:** Constable Edda Rourke
- **Where to go next:** Downhill → Lamplight Row; Up the marble steps → Grand Hall of the Ashcombe Mercantile Bank; North along the bay road → Mooring Mast Nine

**Grand Hall of the Ashcombe Mercantile Bank**
The public hall of the bank, transformed tonight into a ballroom. Chandeliers of cut glass hang above a polished floor, a string quartet plays near the counters, and waiters carry silver trays of champagne. Portraits of three generations of Ashcombes watch from the walls. A sweeping staircase rises to the upper floor; a plain service door behind the counters leads down to the cellars; a pneumatic post terminal clanks behind the head teller's desk.
- **What to take:** glass of champagne; silver serving tray
- **Who to meet:** Silas Ashcombe (greeting his guests until midnight)
- **Where to go next:** Down the marble steps → Ashcombe Square; Up the grand staircase → Clerks' Gallery; Through the service door and down the iron stairs → Boiler Undercroft

**Clerks' Gallery**
A long upper-floor gallery of tall desks and green-shaded lamps, where the bank's clerks copy ledgers by hand. Tonight it is almost empty and very quiet; the music from the ballroom drifts up the stairs. Shelves of leather-bound ledgers cover one wall, and at the far end a frosted-glass door is painted in gold letters: DIRECTOR.
- **What to take:** Founders' Ledger (the bank's first ledger; its opening page records the founding night with an astronomical drawing: the Comet in the house of the Third Moon, the Iron Planet at its highest, the Pale Planet hidden behind the Sun)
- **Who to meet:** Horace Pell
- **Where to go next:** Down the grand staircase → Grand Hall of the Ashcombe Mercantile Bank; Through the frosted-glass door → Director's Office

**Director's Office**
An oak-panelled office that smells of cigars and lamp oil. Above the desk hangs a painting of the original Ashcombe Bank, and behind the painting is a small wall safe with a combination dial. The desk drawers are locked. A window looks out over the bay, toward the distant lights of the mooring masts.
- **What to take:** brass punch card (hidden in the wall safe; the director's master card, which any automaton in the bank will obey); silver letter opener; director's appointment book (notes that the safe combination is "the year of my grandfather's founding" — 1823)
- **Who to meet:** none
- **Where to go next:** Back through the frosted-glass door → Clerks' Gallery

**Boiler Undercroft**
A hot, roaring cellar under the bank where three great boilers feed steam to the radiators, the lifts and the vault's machinery. Pipes of copper and iron run across the ceiling like the roots of a metal tree. A board of pressure gauges and red valve wheels controls the flow of steam to every part of the building. A heavy door marked VAULT — AUTHORISED ONLY leads deeper underground. In the floor, a grated drain opens onto the old sewer below.
- **What to take:** steam valve key (an iron key that turns the main pressure valve; a sharp surge of steam confuses every automaton in the building for a minute); oily rag; engineer's goggles
- **Who to meet:** Bertha Koss
- **Where to go next:** Up the iron stairs → Grand Hall of the Ashcombe Mercantile Bank; Through the vault door → Orrery Vault Antechamber; Down through the floor drain → Old Sewer Culvert

**Old Sewer Culvert**
A brick tunnel half-filled with black water, older than the city above it. Rats watch from the ledges. One branch climbs to the iron hatch on Lamplight Row; another rises to a grated drain under the bank's boiler room. Smugglers once used this tunnel, and their chalk marks still show the way.
- **What to take:** smuggler's chalk
- **Who to meet:** none
- **Where to go next:** Up the ladder to the iron hatch → Lamplight Row; Up through the grated drain → Boiler Undercroft

**Orrery Vault Antechamber**
A round chamber of riveted brass, lit by a single green lamp. In its centre stands the Orrery Lock: a brass model of the heavens taller than a man, with a sun, three moons, the Iron Planet, the Pale Planet and a silver comet on long jointed arms. The great round door of the vault is set into the far wall, and it opens only when the orrery's bodies are moved into the right positions. The positions are those of the night the bank was founded; a wrong setting rings an alarm bell in the ballroom above. Beside the door stands Sentinel Unit Seven, a clockwork guard.
- **What to take:** none
- **Who to meet:** Sentinel Unit Seven
- **Where to go next:** Back through the vault door → Boiler Undercroft; Through the great round door (once the Orrery Lock is solved) → The Orrery Vault

**The Orrery Vault**
A small, silent room behind the great round door, lined with velvet-covered shelves of deeds, bonds and sealed boxes. On a pedestal in the middle, under a glass dome, the Clockheart turns slowly and gives off a soft golden light and a sound like a heartbeat. In the corner, a narrow emergency chute drops into darkness; it is a one-way drop, too steep and slick to climb back up.
- **What to take:** Clockheart (an ever-turning engine of crystal and gold; heavy, warm, and impossible to hide from anyone who hears its ticking); bundle of bearer bonds (worth two hundred crowns)
- **Who to meet:** none
- **Where to go next:** Back through the great round door → Orrery Vault Antechamber; Down the emergency chute (a one-way drop) → Old Sewer Culvert

**Mooring Mast Nine**
An iron mooring mast at the north end of the bay road, rising high above the smog. A rattling cage lift climbs to the top platform, where the airship Wren's Promise strains at its ropes, its engines idling. The wind is cold and smells of coal and salt. From here the whole city is a carpet of lamps.
- **What to take:** none
- **Who to meet:** Captain Juno Vale
- **Where to go next:** South along the bay road → Ashcombe Square
